`rtk discover` over this entire session (1,017 shell commands across several days) estimates
**≈ 68 K tokens** that could have been saved. That is well under 1 % of the session's total
token use, which is dominated by contact-sheet images, file writes and context. Our commands
are already trimmed with `tail`, `grep` and `head`.

**Decision:** RTK works and is safe (telemetry off by default and disabled here). But the saving
is marginal for this workflow, and in our test its pytest filter hid the assertion detail of a
failure, which breaks our rule that diagnostics are never lost. **The hook is not adopted.** RTK
may be used manually as `rtk log <file>` to summarise long render logs, which kept every warning
class. Nothing was installed persistently: the binary lived in the ephemeral scratchpad.
