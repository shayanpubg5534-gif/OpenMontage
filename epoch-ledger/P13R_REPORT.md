## 4. Review limitations (transparent)

I cannot watch video in real time. My review is the closest approximation I have, and it is not a substitute for your
viewing:
- the continuous 10 fps measurements of the whole film (picture changes, holds, motion);
- contact sheets every 1.5–3 s across all 78 s;
- extra frames at every transition and label;
- a full-resolution check of small details. That check is how I found the unreadable FOR SALE sign through the window.

I cannot judge from frames how the voice, the caption rhythm and the motion feel together. That is why the
standard's final check is your first viewing at normal speed.
