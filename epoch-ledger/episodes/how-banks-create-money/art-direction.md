# Art Direction — How Banks Create Money (30 s test)

**Design read:** warm editorial illustration meets a banker's ledger. Hand-authored vector,
outlined cartoon characters with two-tone shading, layered parallax sets, brass-and-ink palette.

**Palette:** ink navy #14213d · parchment #f4ead5 · brass #d4a24c · ledger green #2f7d5b ·
teal #1f7a78 · coral #e0603a · sky #9cc9e6 → dawn #f6c59a.

**Type:** Fraunces 800/600 (display, numbers) · Inter 700/500 (labels).

**Motion:** slow camera drifts (scale 1.00→1.06), parallax by layer depth, back.out pops for
labels, power3 for camera, steps for stamps, linear for 3D orbit. ≥3 easings.

**Signature device:** the double-entry ink line — drawn simultaneously into both columns of the
bank's ledger (scene 4 only).
