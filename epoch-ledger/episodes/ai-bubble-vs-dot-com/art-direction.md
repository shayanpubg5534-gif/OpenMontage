# Art Direction — Is the AI Bubble Bigger Than the Dot-Com Bubble?

Series look is fixed by `epoch-ledger/README.md` (type, caption style, character style).

**Design read:** premium investigative documentary — two eras in dialogue. Warm, nostalgic 1990s
illustration (amber, cream, faded teal, CRT green, light film grain) against cool, metallic modern
AI imagery (navy, steel blue, cyan, brushed metal), bridged by the series ledger palette
(parchment, ink, brass) for analysis beats.

**Signature device (opening and finale only):** the brass ledger line that splits the frame
between 1999 and 2026 (s006) and returns with Dan and Maya facing each other across it (s094).

**Scenes:** see `storyboard.md` (68 scenes, primary subject per scene, word-synced beats).

**Rules:** no logo intro; chapter cards overlay live imagery for ≤ 2 s; every statistic carries a
source label; hypotheticals, forecasts and scenarios are labelled on screen; text ≥ 34 px
(sources ≥ 28 px) and clear of the caption band (y ≥ 880).
