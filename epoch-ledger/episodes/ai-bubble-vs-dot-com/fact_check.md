## GDP comparison — annual basis confirmed

The ECB box states both figures as **annual** shares of US GDP: "On an annual basis, overall AI investment needs would amount to more than 3% of US GDP between 2025 and 2032" and "annual capital expenditure for the development of the Northern Pacific Railway amounted to about 2.24% of US GDP between 1870 and 1890 and the internet boom between 1996 and 2003 took up 1.10% of US GDP". The refined narration ("more than three percent … each year from 2025 to 2032"; "the internet boom between 1996 and 2003 took up about one point one percent") matches. The underlying NBER paper (Van Nieuwerburgh, "Financing the AI Buildout") was not opened — nber.org is not on the approved host list; the ECB's own wording is explicit.
