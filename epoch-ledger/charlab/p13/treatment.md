## q7 (hero): The payments dry up, the securities collapse,

- **Understand:** missing payments shrink the flow and losses climb from the bottom tranche up; senior pieces are hit last but fall in value
- **Feel:** the structure failing
- **Human anchor:** their house, now red in the foundation

| Concept | Kind | Idea | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| literal baseline | literal | routes go grey and a value bar drops (P12) | **rejected** | it implies every investor lost the same way at once, which is inaccurate |
| the flood | metaphor | the pipe's flow thins; red rises from the foundation through equity into mezzanine; senior floors stay dry but their lights dim and they are marked 'downgraded' | **chosen** | loss allocation in its true order, in the same building, with their house among the red ones |

**Accuracy:** Losses hit the most junior tranche first and reach senior tranches only when they exceed the subordination below. Do not show the senior floors destroyed by the first missed payments: in 2007–08 most senior pieces were downgraded and fell in market value, and losses came later and unevenly. 'Collapse' in the narration is shown as market value and ratings collapsing (overlay), not every floor flooding.

**Transitions:** → q8: the camera sinks into the flooded foundation, then cuts to the kitchen table (match on the red)
